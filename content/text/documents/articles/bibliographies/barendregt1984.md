## Citation

Henk Barendregt. *The Lambda Calculus: Its Syntax and Semantics*. Revised
edition, North-Holland, 1984.

## Why this is central

The active mathematics bibliography uses Barendregt as its standard reference
for lambda-term formation, substitution, reduction, and equivalence. This
section cites it to bound the formal-language sense of *term*.

## What it does not settle

Lambda-calculus syntax does not define a terminological designation, a
community's concept, or a glossary carrier. Curry–Howard–Lambek correspondences
among typed terms, proofs, and morphisms do not erase that boundary.
