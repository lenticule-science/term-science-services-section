## Citation

Bernhard Ganter and Rudolf Wille. *Formal Concept Analysis: Mathematical
Foundations*. Springer, 1999.

## Why this is central

This is the standard source named by the repository's active formal-concept
analysis carriers for formal contexts, derivation operators, closed
extent–intent pairs, and concept lattices. It supplies the precise mathematical
sense of *formal concept* used in the comparison with terminological concepts.

## What it does not settle

Formal concept analysis derives closed pairs from incidence data. It does not
by itself select a community's designation, establish synonymy, or define the
record and carrier used to document a glossary term.
